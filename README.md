## How I build

<table width="100%">
  <tr valign="top">
    <td width="33%">
      <sub><b>01 / BUILD</b></sub><br /><br />
      <strong>Start with something worth using.</strong><br />
      <sub>I prefer real products and real constraints over portfolio demos.</sub>
    </td>
    <td width="33%">
      <sub><b>02 / BREAK</b></sub><br /><br />
      <strong>Go after the ugly paths.</strong><br />
      <sub>Memory pressure, flaky networks, retries, caches, device quirks and state that fails at the worst time.</sub>
    </td>
    <td width="33%">
      <sub><b>03 / PROVE</b></sub><br /><br />
      <strong>Evidence beats confidence.</strong><br />
      <sub>Test the thing that matters, keep the diff understandable and do not call it fixed because it looks plausible.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## About

I'm Luca. I like taking rough ideas and turning them into software that feels deliberate.

The problems that usually keep me interested are the ones that work **almost** perfectly: a player that is smooth on one device and slowly grows memory on another, a download that succeeds nine times out of ten, a resolver that is fast until an upstream service behaves badly, or a UI that technically works but still feels one pass away from being right.

I would rather understand the failure and make the smallest useful fix than hide it behind a rewrite. The goal is not just to make something pass once; it is to leave the product and the codebase easier to trust than before.
